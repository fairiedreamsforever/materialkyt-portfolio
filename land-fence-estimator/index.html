    How Acreage Perimeter Costs Are Derived

    One square acre consists of exactly 43,560 square feet. If a parcel tract forms a perfect geometrical square, the mathematical boundaries yield approximately 835 linear feet of perimeter line. However, non-uniform land layouts, shifting boundary configurations, and agricultural corridors typically extend actual fencing runs. Material layout calculations should incorporate an additional 10% scrap margin to accommodate corner bracing setups, structural post offsets, and terrain undulations.
