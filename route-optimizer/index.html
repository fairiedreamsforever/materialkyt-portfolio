How Sequence Re-sorting Lowers Fleet Mileage Friction

Logistics route efficiency relies on a concept known mathematically as the Traveling Salesperson Problem. Standard gig app delivery assignments frequently group multi-order drops in chronological order of checkout times rather than physical geographic alignment. Sorting locations based on direct node-to-node proximity paths reduces unnecessary backtrack loops, driving down active engine wear metrics and vehicle structural depreciation ratios.
