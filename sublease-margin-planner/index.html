    Understanding Commercial Sublease & Arbitrage Structures

    Commercial lease arbitrage involves securing a real estate footprint under a primary master agreement and subsequently partitioning that square footage to micro-tenants. Maximizing profitability relies entirely on driving unit density—such as dedicated desks, coworking zones, or studio footprints—above your raw monthly base rent and operational utility costs. Factoring in systematic vacancy allocations protects asset portfolios from sudden market fluctuations and overhead margin compression.
